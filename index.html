💡 What is Equal-Width Knotting?

Knots ($\xi_1, \xi_2, \dots, \xi_K$) divide the entire range of $X$ from $X_{\min}$ to $X_{\max}$ into equal-width sub-intervals. A separate linear slope is estimated for each segment!

Statistical Modeling Concepts

Piecewise Constant vs. Polynomial Regression

Both methods extend standard linear regression to model non-linear relationships. Piecewise Constant Regression divides the domain into discrete segments, while Polynomial Regression uses higher powers of $X$ to fit a smooth global curve.

1. Piecewise Constant

📊

Partitions domain into segments and fits arithmetic mean $\bar{Y}_k$.

$$\hat{Y}(x) = \sum \bar{Y}_k I(x \in S_k)$$

2. Polynomial Regression

📈

Fits a single smooth polynomial curve of degree $d$ across all $X$.

$$\hat{Y}(x) = \sum_{j=0}^{d} \beta_j x^j$$

3. Spline Regression

〰️

Continuous piecewise polynomials of degree $d$ connected at knots $\xi_k$.

$$\hat{Y}(x) = \sum_{j=0}^{d} \beta_j x^j + \sum_{k=1}^{K} \gamma_k (x - \xi_k)_+^d$$
